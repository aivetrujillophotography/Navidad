What to bring

Little details, big photos

I email you all of this when you book, but here is the summary so you can start planning.

Their favorite book ✦

Yes, bring it 💙

  • Their favorite book or toy

    So little ones feel at ease. Soft colors work best.

  • Cozy textures

    Knits, wool, suede, velvet. They look beautiful with the set light.

  • A 2 or 3 color palette

    Cream, camel, burgundy, deep green, warm gray. Coordinated without matching exactly.

  • Something themed, if you like!

    Christmas pajamas, Grinch sweaters or Santa hats are welcome.

Better not

  • Loud colors

    Neon or very bright colors steal the attention from you (unless it is themed).

  • Brands and logos

    No brand names on clothes, so the focus stays on you.

  • Non Christmas prints

    Cartoons or prints that are not Christmas themed distract from the photo.

Questions

All clear, no fine print.

Where is the studio?

Downtown Ciudad Cuauhtémoc. I send the exact location by WhatsApp with your confirmation. Parking nearby and a bathroom, easy with kids.

Are the gallery photos from this year's set?

They are from previous seasons. The 2026 set is new and I will post real photos as soon as it is built.

Do they work for holiday cards?

Yes! If you want them for cards, book in November or the first week of December so they arrive in time.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, small dogs. Because of the set size, large breeds can't come.

My kids won't sit still

That's perfect. Kids aren't asked to pose: we play, wait for their moment and if we need to stop for food or a nap, we stop.

How many people fit?

Copito is for 1 to 4 people, Trineo for 5 to 6 and Reno for 7 to 9 (20 photos, $2,100). Babies and under 3s are held. 10 or more? Let's talk, it depends on ages and sizes.

How do I book my date?

With $500 by bank transfer. The rest on session day, cash or transfer.

What should we wear?

Layers and textures: wool, chunky knits, suede. Colors like camel, cream, burgundy, deep green or warm gray, muted rather than bright. I send my guide when you book.

When do I get my photos?

I send you the gallery the same day or the next so you can pick your favorites. Then I hand edit them one by one so you have them by the 24th at the latest, ready for Christmas Eve.

Meet Aivé

Christmas in Cuauhtémoc, my home

I grew up in Cuauhtémoc and that is where I have my studio. Every Christmas I come back to do these sessions and I love it: it is the most beautiful time of the year.

What I love most is seeing the same families come back year after year: kids I photographed as babies, now running around the set. Watching them grow up in front of my camera, I love it!

In the studio everything is ready and cozy: we chat, we laugh, kids play, babies take their time and I gently guide you so you just enjoy it.
